Gnome Village – Privacy Policy

Applies to Gnome Village and Gnome Village Plus on Android, iOS and desktop. Last updated: 3 November 2026.

The short version

Gnome Village has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. We do not collect, sell or share your personal information. The free edition shows one Google AdMob advertisement while the game is paused, and Google handles the data needed for that ad as described below. Gnome Village Plus shows no ads and sends no data anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your saved isles and settings (sound, speed, whether you have seen the tutorial) are stored only on your device. On Android, the system's own backup may copy them to your Google account if you have backup turned on; on iPhone, iCloud backup may do the same. On Steam, saves can travel between your computers through Steam Cloud. We cannot see any of this.

Advertising in the free edition

The free edition uses Google AdMob to show a banner while the game is paused. To show, limit, and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version), and
  • how you interact with the ad (views and taps).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or another region where the law requires it, you are asked for your choice before any ad is requested. You can change that choice at any time from the game's pause menu: About & privacy → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Gnome Village Plus

The paid edition contains no advertising and collects nothing. The internet is used only if you choose to open a store link.

Children

The game itself collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Ads in the free edition are provided by Google AdMob under the settings described above.

Data deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything the game stored, delete your saves from the Load screen or uninstall the app.
